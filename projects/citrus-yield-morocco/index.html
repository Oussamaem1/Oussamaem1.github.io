Citrus Yield Prediction in Morocco

A tabular regression problem set in Moroccan agriculture: estimating citrus yield from rainfall records, soil characteristics and planted acreage, approached as a Kaggle competition.

Period
Academic project · Kaggle competition

Problem

Yield depends on weather, soil and how much land is planted, but the relationship is noisy and regional. The task was to predict citrus yield in Morocco from those inputs.

Approach

  • Feature preparation over rainfall, soil and acreage records.
  • Gradient-boosted and classical regression models evaluated against the competition metric.

Tools

  • Python
  • Scikit-learn
  • XGBoost
  • Pandas
