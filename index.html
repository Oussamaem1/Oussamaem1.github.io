04 / About

A bit of background

I am a Data Scientist at Cleverlytics, where my work spans three streams: geological AI from mid-wave infrared spectral signatures for a major mining group, a vision and LiDAR collision-avoidance system for open-pit vehicles, and Darija speech synthesis for a banking voice assistant.

Before that I spent a research internship at LAMMOC, Universidade Federal Fluminense in Rio de Janeiro, on the SALAD national project, building rainfall-based landslide early warning across several Brazilian regions. My engineering degree at EMINES – UM6P combined industrial management with a data science track, which is why most of my work sits where modelling meets an actual operational process.

What I enjoy most is the full path from raw sensor or spectral data to something a team can use — a benchmark that settles a modelling question, a service running inside an information system, or an alert a driver can act on.
